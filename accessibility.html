A

This statement

This statement applies to the Cloud Nine Clinical website at {{SITE_DOMAIN}}. Our aim is for it to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

B

Using this website

You should be able to:

  • zoom in up to 400% without text spilling off the screen;
  • change colours, contrast and fonts using your browser or device settings;
  • reach every link, button and form field using only a keyboard, with a visible focus outline;
  • use the website with a screen reader: every page has headings, landmarks and a link to skip to the content;
  • stop the walk on the home page by pressing Reduce motion, which the site remembers, or pass it with Skip the walk;
  • fill in the consultation form with errors explained in words, next to the field and in a summary at the top.

C

What we know does not work yet

  • Motion. The walk on the home page plays even if your device is set to reduce motion. It stops as soon as you press Reduce motion, and stays stopped on later visits.
  • The walk's photographs have no text description. They are decorative: everything the walk says is also in the text of the page below it.
  • Unfinished details. Some text is still waiting for the clinic's details, and shows a bracketed field instead.

The website has not yet been audited by an independent accessibility specialist. That audit is planned for {{AUDIT_DATE}}, and this list will be updated with what it finds.

F

If you are not happy with our response

Tell us first, through our complaints procedure. Under the Equality Act 2010 we must make reasonable adjustments so that disabled people can use our service. If you are still not happy, the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS) can advise you on what to do next.

G

Technical information

This website is built as plain HTML pages, designed to work in current versions of the main browsers, on phones and computers, with or without a mouse. The pages do not depend on the walk: with motion reduced, everything is still there as text.
