B

What we collect

  • Your contact details: name, address, date of birth, phone number and email address.
  • Your health information: your medical history, consultation notes, prescriptions, and letters to and from your GP and other clinicians. The law calls this special category data and gives it extra protection.
  • Your GP's details, and your referral if you have one.
  • Proof of identity, where we need to confirm who you are before prescribing.
  • Payment records: what you paid and when. Card payments are handled by {{PAYMENT_PROVIDER}}; we never see or keep your full card number.
  • What you tell us when you get in touch: the consultation request form, emails and phone calls.

C

Why we use it, and the law that allows it

  • To answer your consultation request and arrange an appointment. Lawful basis: steps you have asked us to take before a contract, UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b).
  • To provide your care. Lawful basis: our contract with you, Article 6(1)(b). For your health information: the provision of health care by, or under the responsibility of, a health professional who owes you a duty of confidentiality, Article 9(2)(h) and Schedule 1 of the Data Protection Act 2018.
  • To meet our legal duties, such as keeping clinical and prescription records and answering our regulators. Lawful basis: legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c).
  • To run the clinic safely and well: clinical audit, handling complaints, and checking the quality of our service. Lawful basis: legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f), and Article 9(2)(h) where health information is involved.
  • To send you news from the clinic, only if you have asked for it. Lawful basis: consent, Article 6(1)(a). You can stop at any time.

We never use your health information for marketing. No decision about your care is made by a computer alone.

D

Who we share it with

  • Your GP, with your consent. If you would rather your GP was not told, discuss it with your clinician: for some prescriptions, sharing with your GP is part of prescribing safely.
  • The pharmacy that dispenses a prescription written for you.
  • Other clinicians involved in your care, with your agreement.
  • The companies that run our systems: clinical records ({{CLINICAL_SYSTEM}}), email, telephone and payments. They act only on our instructions, under contract, and may not use your information for anything else.
  • Regulators and public bodies, where the law requires it: for example the Care Quality Commission, the General Medical Council, or a court.

We never sell your information.

G

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us:

  • for a copy of the information we hold about you (a subject access request). It is free, and we answer within one month;
  • to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • to delete information, although we must keep medical records for the periods above;
  • to restrict, or object to, how we use it;
  • to move it to another provider, where that right applies;
  • to stop using it, where we rely on your consent.

To use any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at {{DPO_EMAIL}}. We may ask you to confirm who you are first, to protect your records.

H

Keeping it safe

Access to your records is limited to the people who need it for your care or to run the clinic, and our staff are trained in confidentiality. If something goes wrong with your information, we will tell you, and we will report it to the Information Commissioner's Office within 72 hours where the law requires.

I

This website

This website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. It keeps a few small settings in your own browser, which are never sent to us: whether you chose to reduce motion on the home page, where you were on the home page when you left it, and, in the demonstration patient area, that you have signed in and the email address you typed. Only the motion setting lasts beyond the browser tab.

The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when a page loads.

This is a demonstration site. The consultation request form and the patient sign-in send nothing anywhere.
