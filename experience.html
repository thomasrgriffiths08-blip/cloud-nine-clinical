01 — What happens

The consultation, step by step.

No forms to fight, no counter to reach. A conversation, a decision, and a plan written down.

Request an initial consultation →

{{DISCLOSURE}} A consultation does not guarantee any particular treatment; that decision is made by a clinician, with you.

  1. I

    You get in touch.

    By phone or the form. We ask a few questions to make sure we are the right clinic for you.

  2. II

    The consultation.

    With a specialist. Your history, what you have tried, what you want to change.

  3. III

    A written plan.

    What was decided and why, in plain words, sent to you and, with your consent, your GP.

  4. IV

    Follow-up.

    Reviewed at {{REVIEW_INTERVAL}}. Adjusted if it needs to be. Stopped if it should be.
